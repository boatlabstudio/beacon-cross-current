AI Learns to Drive a Boat | Beacon Episode 01

Beacon has one job: get through a six-metre buoy gate while the current pushes sideways. The first 169 training runs go wrong in different ways. On run 170, something clicks. The video shows the attempts, the changing signals inside the network, and the full crossing from the bow.

GitHub (Unity project, PPO training code, checkpoints, and replay data): [REPOSITORY URL]

First clean training crossing: attempt 170.
First recorded checkpoint with 48/48 fixed tests: after 1,278 completed training episodes.
Final checkpoint: 48/48, with the featured crossing taking 8.80 seconds.
